Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Midnight Archives (the "app") is a private tool used by the author of the Midnight Archives YouTube channel to publish the author's own videos. It is not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

When the author connects a YouTube channel, the app asks Google for permission to manage that channel through the YouTube Data API. It uses this permission only to:

  • upload videos created by the author to the author's channel;
  • set the thumbnail, title, description and tags of those videos;
  • post and pin a comment under those videos;
  • read the channel's name and branding so the author can see which channel is connected.

How data is stored

The access token Google issues is stored only on the author's own computer. The app has no server, does not collect analytics and does not store data about viewers or any other Google users.

Sharing

No data obtained through Google APIs is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page. Deleting the token from the author's computer also disconnects the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen of the app.
